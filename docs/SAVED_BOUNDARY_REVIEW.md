# Saved declared-boundary review

Checker schematic-1.5.0 adds a narrow saved review of deliberately declared schematic
boundary rings. The broader room/door/area/adjacency coverage remains unchecked. A
passing declared ring is not a complete room inventory, physical enclosure or calibrated
area measurement. No automatic declarations or geometry corrections are made.

The site uses the same guarded source evidence and topology evaluator as standalone
boundary preview. Empty, open-plan, stale, missing, unsupported and ambiguous evidence
remains unknown. Supported conflicts remain visible without converting incomplete
evidence into a pass. Saved records bind project/revision/state/source/checker/time.

Historical checker1.0 through1.4.1 report formats retain their exact capability and
coverage contracts. They continue to import and become stale under the newer engine;
rerun review rather than relabelling old evidence.

The current run can be compared, highlighted where trusted current segment references
exist, exported privately and retained in project backups/manual handoffs. Missing or
stale references cannot grant trustworthy plan highlights. Reports retain bounded
evidence and disclose incomplete coverage rather than silently claiming completeness.

Actual source/survey/code checks, room discovery, measured areas, adjacency and hosted
door swings/clearances remain separate requirements.

Evidence budgets retain at most 100 declarations and 4096 declared edges per report,
with at most 2,000,000 serialized context characters. Omitted findings require an
incomplete-review finding and unknown declared-boundary coverage. Imported proof is
replayed through the same evaluator; modified outcomes, counts and references reject.
Violation samples disclose their total when truncated.

Verification includes the full 50-command suite, independent replay/tamper review and
fabricated native save/export/import/comparison/highlight acceptance. Historical saved
reports remain unchanged and cannot highlight stale references. Actual household
browser acceptance is separately gated; generic tests do not certify physical facts.
